<p style="margin-top: 1.4rem;">I am a co-founder and <strong>Fund X Managing Partner</strong> of Orion Alpha Asset Management. The firm's core belief is that durable alpha comes from pairing the business judgment of a fundamental analyst with the statistical rigor of a quantitative researcher — each one disciplining the other. Fundamental theses are stress-tested against data before capital is committed; quantitative signals are interrogated for an economic rationale before they are trusted.</p>

## *Strategy Pillars* {#strategy}

<div class="pillar-grid">
  <div class="pillar">
    <div class="pillar-num">I</div>
    <div class="pillar-title">Fundamental Conviction</div>
    <div class="pillar-body">Bottom-up equity research: business quality, unit economics, competitive dynamics, and management execution — the qualitative edge a model can't see.</div>
  </div>
  <div class="pillar">
    <div class="pillar-num">II</div>
    <div class="pillar-title">Bayesian Validation</div>
    <div class="pillar-body">Every thesis is treated as a prior. Market data, positioning, and earnings outcomes update conviction systematically — eliminating narrative bias.</div>
  </div>
  <div class="pillar">
    <div class="pillar-num">III</div>
    <div class="pillar-title">Derivatives &amp; Structure</div>
    <div class="pillar-body">Options and volatility structures express views with defined risk, monetize uncertainty around catalysts, and shape the return distribution.</div>
  </div>
  <div class="pillar">
    <div class="pillar-num">IV</div>
    <div class="pillar-title">Risk Discipline</div>
    <div class="pillar-body">Position sizing, drawdown limits, and regime awareness come before return targets. The first objective is staying in the game.</div>
  </div>
</div>
