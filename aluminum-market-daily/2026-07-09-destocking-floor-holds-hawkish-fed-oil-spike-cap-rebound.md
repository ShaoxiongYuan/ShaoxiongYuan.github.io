## 3 · Inventory

<span class="lbl lbl-data">Data</span>

- Domestic ingot social inventory is in the **~85.9–87.2 kt** consumption-area range; the prior week saw the fastest destocking in ~4 years, with ingot warehouse withdrawals at a multi-year high.
- More recent tracking shows the destocking pace **decelerating** into the off-season; weekly change swung toward a small build (~+0.5 kt vs a prior ~+2.4 kt), i.e. a fragile supply/low-price-demand balance forming near the 22,400–22,900 CNY/mt zone.
- Liquid-aluminum share still elevated; casting-ingot output subdued; aluminum-rod inventory showed some drawdown on low-price restocking.

<span class="lbl lbl-view">View</span>

- The four-year-high destocking remains the single most important bullish support and the floor under SHFE; **but the pace is slowing**, so the floor is holding rather than driving prices higher.
- Absolute inventory is still elevated; with off-season demand plus more casting ingots, renewed buildup is a live risk. Inventory **cushions, it is not a strong driver**; watch whether withdrawals re-accelerate.

- Fundamental drivers remain a tug-of-war; price is dominated by "destocking floor / ex-China premiums vs off-season + supply increment + hawkish macro."

<span class="lbl lbl-view">解读</span>

- 若去库重新提速、海外升水维持强势，贴水有望进一步企稳；美元走强与淡季需求限制近端上行。

## 七 · 关键价格驱动

**<span class="tone-bull">利多</span>**

- 四年高位去库使国内库存仍偏低（为沪铝提供底部）。
- 海外区域升水维持高位（日本 MJP、欧洲、美中西部）。
- 油价急涨（Brent +5.2%、WTI +4.4%）抬升成本/电力成本预期；中东供应风险溢价。
- 中国 6 月 PMI 重回扩张（50.3）；新出口订单 >50；铝材出口偏韧。
- LME 站上 MA5，超跌动能减弱。

**<span class="tone-bear">利空</span>**

- 美联储更趋鹰派（9月加息概率约 70%；核心 PCE ~3.4% / CPI ~4.2%）；美元走强。
- 氧化铝各地区指数全线下跌 → 成本支撑走弱。
- 去库节奏放缓；淡季存在再累库风险。
- 海外（印尼/中东）及国内新增/复产产能释放；地产/建筑偏弱。
- 中长期均线（沪铝 MA40/60、LME 全周期）仍偏空；下游需求偏弱。

## 八 · 一句话观点
