## 3 · Inventory

<span class="lbl lbl-data">Data</span>

- **LME stocks fell below 300,000 t for the first time since 2022** (~298,775 t as at Jul 6, from ~318,000 t in early June); a material physical tightening and the session's key bullish catalyst.
- **Domestic ingot social inventory:** three-week destocking of **>140 kt** earlier, but the pace has **decelerated** into the off-season, with the latest weekly change a small build (~+5 kt vs a prior ~+24 kt).
- **Aluminum rod inventory:** ~130 kt as at Jul 2, **−10 kt w/w**, near a three-year seasonal low on low-price restocking.

<span class="lbl lbl-view">View</span>

- The story is now **two-sided-bullish on inventory**: LME's sub-300 kt milestone reignites the global-deficit narrative, while China's low ingot inventory keeps the SHFE floor intact.
- Caveat: domestic destocking is **slowing** and casting-ingot supply could rebuild inventory into the off-season, so the domestic leg is supportive rather than a strong upward driver. Watch whether LME draws and Chinese withdrawals both persist.

<span class="lbl lbl-view">View: impact on aluminum</span>

- **<span class="tone-bull">Bullish:</span>** steady-to-softer dollar; China PMI in expansion + export orders recovering; policy sentiment.
- **<span class="tone-bear">Bearish:</span>** still-elevated September-hike odds (~70%) and sticky core inflation; a divided Fed that could re-hawkish on any hot data.
- **Net:** mixed but **modestly less of a headwind than the prior session**; the swing factors are the dollar's direction (break below 100.4 vs reclaim 101.2+) and whether September-hike pricing holds near 70%.
