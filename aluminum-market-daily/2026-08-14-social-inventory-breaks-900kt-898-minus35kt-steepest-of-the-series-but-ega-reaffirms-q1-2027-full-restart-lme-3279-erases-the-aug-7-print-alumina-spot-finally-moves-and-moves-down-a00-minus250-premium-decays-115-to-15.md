headline: "All three of yesterday's falsifiable tests were answered inside one session, and the two that the bull case needed most were answered in its favour — yet the market fell anyway, because a fourth item arrived that neither test was built to catch. The inventory test passed outright and passed big: SMM's Thursday social-inventory print landed at 898,000 mt, DOWN 35,000 mt against Thursday August 6's 933,000 and down 19,000 against Monday's 917,000 — the steepest Thursday-to-Thursday draw of the series and the FIRST print below 900,000 mt, so the Thursday series now reads 953,000 → 933,000 → 898,000 and the destocking pillar did exactly what this brief asked of it. The basis test passed on the letter and failed on the trend: SMM A00 fell 250 yuan to 24,120 while AL2608 fell only 150 to 24,105, so the East China premium SURVIVED at +15 rather than snapping back to the −100 discount band — but it survived having given up 100 of its 115 points in a single session, which is a decaying premium, not a confirmed bid. The alumina test was answered too, on the third session, and it was answered DOWNWARD: the SMM alumina index printed 2,690.35, −2.53, with Shandong, Henan, Shanxi and Guangxi each cutting 5 yuan to 2,695, 2,730, 2,720 and 2,615 and only Guizhou unchanged at 2,765 — so after two flat sessions the first real move Chinese alumina spot made following Alunorte was a move against it, and alumina futures went with it, AO2610 closing 2,680 against 2,711 on an open-interest build of 35,188 and product open interest up 33,771, the largest build of the sequence and the mirror image of the previous session's liquidation. What none of yesterday's tests were built to catch is the item that Reuters attributes both of the last two LME declines to: Emirates Global Aluminium reaffirmed that its war-damaged Al Taweelah smelter, currently running at 18% of capacity, will return to pre-incident output levels in the FIRST QUARTER OF 2027, with restoration capex of AED 1.5 billion and the recycling plant back to full rate by end-2026 — and the European physical premium has already eased to $487/mt from a May peak of $621. Australia added a second supply-preservation headline on August 13: a A$2.5 billion, roughly US$1.8 billion government package for Rio Tinto's Tomago, the country's largest smelter and its single largest electricity user, extending operations ten years from 2029 against a minimum $1.1 billion operator investment. Both legs priced it. LME cash settled August 13 at $3,279.00, DOWN $28.50, and three-month at $3,275.00, down $33.50 — a second consecutive decline totalling $94.00 from the August 11 high, leaving cash 50 CENTS below the August 7 print of $3,279.50 and therefore erasing the entire rebound on the cash caliber; the one constructive detail is that the curve flipped back, the cash-to-three-month spread moving from a $1.00 CONTANGO to a $4.00 BACKWARDATION, on a thirty-third consecutive stock draw to 250,000 mt. SHFE broke down harder. AL2609 opened 24,300 — the exact night-session open — never traded above the night high of 24,335, fell all day to 24,055 and closed 24,090, which is 195 below the previous close, 255 below the previous settlement, 210 below its own open and, for the first time in this rebound, BELOW the MA5 of 24,117. The open-interest question this brief has run all week resolved against the August 12 reading: AL2609 shed 10,631 lots after building 1,895, on volume of 154,596 — so the build that arrived at the cycle high was, on the next session's evidence, length taken at the top. Whole-product open interest nonetheless rose 1,345, because the roll is nearly finished: AL2610 added 6,571 in the day and 6,030 more overnight, and the front-to-second gap has collapsed from 26,839 to 9,637 and then to 211, so AL2610 is now effectively the main contract. The night session that opened 21:00 on August 13 opened ABOVE the day closes for the first time in three nights (+55 / 0 / +15) but traded down to 23,985 — the first sub-24,000 print since the August 7 close of 23,980, and a second consecutive night breaking the day close — while whole-product night open interest built 8,361 on volume up from 91,185 to 116,035. Macro turned friendly for a second straight day and again did not help: July PPI printed UNCHANGED against a +0.2% forecast on a 5.7% drop in producer gasoline, though core ran +0.4%; jobless claims rose to 209,000; DXY finally eased to 99.876 from 99.940; and September hold probability moved to roughly 65% on one caliber against a spread of roughly 40–50% hike odds on others. Brent fell a second session to $87.92 and then to $86.87 live, with the IEA and OPEC both cutting demand forecasts and OPEC's 2026 growth estimate down to 580,000 bpd, a fourth consecutive reduction, even as Trump claimed total control of the Strait of Hormuz and Iran declared it blocked."
