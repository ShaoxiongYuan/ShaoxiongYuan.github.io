headline: "A 6.3 Mt/yr refinery went to half rate and rewrote the cost column overnight. Norsk Hydro announced on August 11 that Alunorte in Pará, Brazil — the largest alumina refinery outside China — has cut production to 50% of capacity after its gas supplier CELBA, part of New Fortress Group, disrupted natural gas availability; the company is buying spot gas, has requested direct access to the Barcarena LNG terminal, estimates a Q3 2026 hit of $75–100 million to its Bauxite & Alumina segment, and gave no restoration date beyond 'as soon as gas availability normalizes.' At 50% of 6.3 Mt/yr the run-rate loss is roughly 3.15 Mt annualized, about 8,630 mt/day. That single item is why today's brief reads differently from yesterday's, and it landed on a session that had already answered yesterday's question in the affirmative. SHFE AL2609 closed August 11 at 24,250, up 215 (+0.89%) on the previous settlement and up 85 on the close, a second consecutive higher close and a new rebound high; AL2610 closed 24,260, +210, and all twelve listed contracts closed at or above 24,150. The night session that opened 21:00 Beijing on August 11 gapped above the day-session close for a SECOND consecutive night and this time on all three actives — AL2609 opened 24,290 (+40), AL2610 24,305 (+45), AL2608 24,270 (+70) — ran to 24,365, and never traded back below the close, its low of 24,270 sitting 20 above it, adding 2,605 lots of open interest. And the physical market, which refused to pay for Monday's breakout, paid for this one: SMM A00 in Shanghai rose 110 to 24,100, with eight of nine regions up 100 to 130 against the 10-to-60 band of the day before, so the East China basis narrowed from −120 to −100, Central China from −220 to −210, Foshan's premium widened from 40 to 50, and East China purchasing sentiment printed 3.20, up 0.04 on the day and up from 2.86 on August 6. LME confirmed in the same direction and cleared the one level that mattered: cash settled $3,373.00, +$45.50, three-month $3,374.00, +$53.50, a seventh consecutive higher session, taking out both MA60 at $3,349.41 and the $3,350 core-range top that this brief flagged yesterday as the single overhead reference; stocks fell to 253,400 mt, a thirty-first straight draw. Against that, three things must be conceded plainly. The $7.00 backwardation this brief called 'the one LME datum positioning cannot explain' lasted exactly one session and flipped back to a $1.00 CONTANGO, with three-month rising more than cash — which is what a forward supply shock looks like, not immediate tightness. Alumina SPOT FELL on the day alumina futures spiked: the SMM index printed 2,692.52, −3.66, with Shandong −10, Henan −5, Shanxi −5 and Guangxi −5, and the futures move was a closing-hour event, AO2610 closing 2,727 a full 38 above its own settlement of 2,689. And AL2609's open interest fell a second consecutive session, −1,415, on a third consecutive decline in volume to 131,151 lots, with the entire net build again in AL2610 (+9,157 day, +4,702 night). Macro turned hawkish underneath all of it — September hike odds moved to roughly 51% on the TradingEconomics caliber and about 55% implied by FedWatch's 45.1% hold, from 46% and 40% a day earlier, after Cleveland Fed President Hammack said several rate increases may be needed — and the Hormuz headlines turned constructive, with Pakistan's defence minister saying the US and Iran are 'close to some sort of arrangement.' US July CPI lands tonight."

## 五 · 基本面

<span class="lbl lbl-data">⚑ 本期核心新增:Alunorte 降至 50% 负荷</span>

- **公告方与日期:**挪威海德鲁(Norsk Hydro),2026年8月11日**。**
- **对象:**巴西帕拉州 Alunorte 氧化铝精炼厂,产能 630 万吨/年,被描述为中国以外全球最大氧化铝精炼厂**。**
- **事由:**供气方 CELBA(New Fortress Group 旗下)通报天然气供应中断**;CELBA 同时持有 Barcarena 终端设施。**
- **措施:**产量临时削减至产能的 50%**;采购现货气源;**已申请直接接入 Barcarena LNG 接收与再气化终端**;同时寻求长期替代气源,并保留现行供气协议下的合同权利。**
- **财务影响:**预计对 2026 年三季度铝土矿与氧化铝板块造成 7,500 万–1 亿美元影响**(减产 + 高于合同价的购气成本)。**
- **时间表:**“待供气恢复正常后即开始爬产”,未给出具体日期**。**
- **本简报推算(**解读**):630 万吨 × 50% ≈ **折年减量 315 万吨,约 8,630 吨/日**;叠加 7月16日美铝下调的 **20–30 万吨**,全球氧化铝平衡的减量证据在一个月内**连续第二次出现**。**

<span class="lbl lbl-data">其余供应(延续)</span>

- **海德鲁 Qatalum(卡塔尔)因伊朗打击中东能源设施后的天然气短缺**停产**;波斯湾地区约占全球产量 **10%**,自 3 月冲突升级以来持续受阻。**
- **境外:7 月中国以外产量同比 **−6.7%**;某机构预计 2026 年海外供应减少 **98 万吨**。**
- **国内:周度产量基本持平,铝水比例上升;7 月产量同比 +1.6%、环比 +3.5%,**铸锭产量同比 −15.1%**;铝水比例 7 月 78.3%、8 月预计 78.5%;建成产能约 4,629 万吨、运行产能 4,531 万吨;**4,500 万吨天花板约束性增强**。**
- **复产:Slovalco 四季度、密苏里年底、EGA Al Taweelah 持续(89/1,262 槽);几内亚上半年铝土矿出口 1.148 亿吨(+15%)。**
- **成本与利润(8月4日口径,延续):平均完全成本 16,252 元/吨,平均利润 6,719 元/吨。**

<span class="lbl lbl-data">需求</span>

- **加工开工率 **60.1% 且下行**;7 月铝加工 PMI **42.2,七个板块全部收缩**;再生铝开工率 49.4%;铝棒加工费回落;传统淡季。**
- **光伏组件排产:7 月 38.5 GW → 8 月 39.3 GW。**
- **⚑ SMM 8月11日明确表述:**24,000 元之上的价格对下游采购构成抑制,成交主要集中于贸易商之间**;华中成交量偏低、下游采购情绪低迷。**
- **但同期**华东采购意愿指数 3.20(+0.04)**,较 8月6日的 2.86 上升 0.34——**两条表述方向相反,并列记录**。**

<span class="lbl lbl-view">解读</span>

- **Alunorte 是本简报所载**单体最大的供应事件**,其性质是**远期减量**:它解释了伦铝三个月涨幅大于现货、以及氧化铝期货尾盘异动。**
- **但**它尚未出现在任何一个现货价格中**:氧化铝现货当日下跌,澳洲 FOB 仅 +1 美元。** **因此该项应记入**成本地板的前瞻栏**,而非当期栏(此为解读)。**
- **需求侧的两条读数**互相冲突**:SMM 描述价格抑制采购,而华东采购意愿指数连续回升。** **可能的调和是**区域分化**(华东改善、华中疲弱),但本简报不作强行调和。**
