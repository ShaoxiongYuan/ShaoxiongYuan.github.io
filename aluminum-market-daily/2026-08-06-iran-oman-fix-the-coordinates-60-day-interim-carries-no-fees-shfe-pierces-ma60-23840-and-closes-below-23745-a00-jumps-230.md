  net: "Yesterday this brief put two falsifiable questions at the top of the watchlist, and both were settled inside one session. The first was whether SHFE could reclaim its 60-day moving average. It did — for part of an afternoon. AL2609 opened at 23,800, ran to 23,840, and traded 80.83 yuan clear of an MA60 at 23,759.17, the first time in this entire rebound that the line was on the wrong side of price. Then it gave all of it back and closed at 23,745, fourteen yuan below. That is a materially worse outcome than never reaching the line at all, because a level that is touched and surrendered has been tested and has held, and the market now knows where the seller is. The second question was whether the spot basis would follow the board up. It did, and it did so emphatically: SMM A00 rose 230 yuan to 23,730 — which also cleanly resolves yesterday's date ambiguity, since 23,500 plus 230 is 23,730, confirming 23,500 as the August 4 print — and the East China basis flipped from a discount of 20-to-parity into a premium of 8 to 40 against the August contract, while ingot inventory in the major consumption areas drew 9,500 mt with Guangdong, Wuxi and Gongyi all destocking together. So the structure of the last three sessions has inverted. Through August 3 to 5 the board led and physical withheld endorsement; on August 5 physical endorsed and the board stalled. That is not a contradiction, it is a handoff, and it matters because the two halves are now pricing different things. The board is pricing the news, and the news got worse. Iran and Oman finalized the geographical coordinates of the proposed route on Wednesday, with the joint statement in final drafting, and the interim terms as now reported are more bearish than the version this brief carried yesterday in the one respect that counts. Yesterday's structure was enter-via-Iran, exit-via-Oman, with service fees for security and marine-environment protection — and the fee was the obstacle, because Gulf states and Washington had rejected tolls outright and Trump has said flatly that he is not going to let them charge. The new interim structure keeps the lanes, drops the fees entirely for sixty days, and hands the whole question of administration, services and associated costs to a joint working group between the two foreign ministries. That is not a resolution of the hardest issue; it is a deferral of it. But deferral is how deals get signed, and a signed deal is what the aluminum price has been paid not to expect. The rest of the interim text is equally concrete: mines cleared from the median lane within thirty days, after which the median lane carries both directions under a permanent arrangement still to be negotiated. Crude has already made its judgment — WTI near $75 after an 11% loss across three sessions, Brent at $79.29 — and the dollar index has fallen to 99.650, the lowest in seven weeks, on coordinated US–Japan yen intervention and on energy-driven disinflation dimming rate-increase expectations. A seven-week low in the dollar is genuinely supportive for the metal and is the cleanest bullish item in today's macro column. Three defences remain and none of them are rhetorical. Iran states that no agreement guarantees safe passage while the United States maintains its naval blockade of Iranian ports, which means the text can be signed and the lane can still be unusable. The Houthis claimed a strike on a Saudi tanker in the Gulf of Aden and threatened further vessels in the Red Sea, which is a second and separate corridor risk that no Hormuz text addresses. And eight vessels transited on Monday against roughly one hundred and thirty a day before the crisis — a 94% shortfall that thirty days of mine clearing does not close on announcement. But the sharpest fact available today is not an argument, it is a precedent. This market has already priced a Hormuz deal once. On the June 17 framework, LME spot aluminum fell from a year-to-date peak of $3,854 on June 2 to $3,418 on June 15, about eleven percent off the high and the lowest since mid-March. And then that framework collapsed on July 8 when Iran struck multiple commercial ships, and the premium came back. So the honest reading is symmetric rather than directional: the market has demonstrated both what a deal costs aluminum and what a broken deal returns to it, and the only genuinely new information in either direction would be mine-clearing actually starting or the blockade actually lifting. Underneath all of it the domestic picture has quietly improved at the margin for the first time in weeks — A00 up 230, the basis flipped to premium, three regions destocking on the day, and SMM's July average weekly drawdown of 54,800 mt confirmed as the strongest for that period in three years with a projection that social inventory could fall below 800,000 mt in the second half — even as the weekly social-inventory series itself has already turned to a 958,000 mt print on a +5,000 mt build. Those two facts are not in conflict: the weekly series measures the turn, the daily regional prints measure the day, and today the day was better than the turn. The technical verdict stands where it stood, only tighter: both boards remain below MA60, SHFE by 14.17 yuan and LME by roughly 4.3%, and SHFE's MA60 is itself falling 13.75 yuan a session, which means the level will come to price even if price does nothing. A rebound that pierces its 60-day and closes back under it has not repaired a downtrend — but it is now close enough that the next session decides it rather than merely approaches it"

<span class="lbl lbl-view">解读</span>

- **氧化铝一侧本期无新印记,所有数值均为延续口径,其“成本下移 + 新增产能 + 矿库累积”的方向未变,但今日不构成新的边际信息。**

<span class="lbl lbl-view">解读:两个频率,两个结论,不应混用</span>

- **必须先澄清一处容易混淆的口径:7 月“周均去库 54,800 吨”与此前引用的“周度出库量 12.77 万吨”不是同一指标。** **前者是社会库存的净减少量,后者是仓库出库量。** **54,800 吨/周的净去库与“7月23日 100.6 万吨 → 7月30日 95.3 万吨”的路径(净减约 5.3 万吨)完全自洽。**
- **因此本期的信息结构是:周频指标已经转向(+5,000 吨),而日频指标在转向之后仍能给出连续的去库读数(−9,500 吨、三地同步)。**
- **正确的读法是:趋势已经拐头,但拐头之后的斜率尚未确立。** **一次周度累库确认了方向的改变;随后的日频去库说明这一改变的幅度可能远比“恢复累库周期”要温和。**
- **SMM 自身给出的“下半年可能跌破 80 万吨”这一展望,与本期 +5,000 吨的累库并列存在,应当被理解为“方向翻转但年内低位未必被打破”的中性偏多设定,而非对累库的否认** (<span class="lbl lbl-view">解读</span>)。
- **境外一侧无任何新信息:LME 库存已连续十二个交易日停留在 7月24日的 271,275 吨口径。** **在缺乏更新的情况下,该数值的“史低”属性仍然成立,但其作为“当期证据”的效力正在随时间衰减,不宜再作为新增利多反复计入。**
