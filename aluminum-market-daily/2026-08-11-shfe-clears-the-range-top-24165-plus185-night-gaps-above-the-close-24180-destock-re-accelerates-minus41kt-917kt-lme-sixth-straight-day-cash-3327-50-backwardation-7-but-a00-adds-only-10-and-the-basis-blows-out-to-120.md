  net: "Yesterday this brief recorded five bearish items and asked one falsifiable question: with 24,100 only 60 yuan away and the night session having opened below the close, does the day session take the range top on the second attempt, or is the failure to gap on payrolls the more informative signal? The session answered, and it answered on the first attempt rather than the second. AL2609 closed 24,165 — 65 above the 24,100 core-range top, 35 above the August 3–7 weekly high of 24,130, and with every listed contract from AL2608 out to AL2707 closing above 24,000. That is not a marginal breach; it is the whole curve repricing. And four of yesterday's five bearish items were reversed within twenty-four hours, which has to be said plainly because this brief put weight on each of them. First, the night session. Yesterday's classification rested on all three actives opening below their day-session closes on the most bullish US labour print of the quarter, and this brief wrote that the result 'formally classifies aluminum's current driver as non-macro.' Monday night did the mirror image: AL2609 opened 24,180 against a close of 24,165, ran to 24,195, and added 949 lots of open interest; AL2610 opened 24,200 against a close of 24,180 and added 3,922. Only AL2608 opened marginally below, by 10. One session does not settle a classification either way, but a test that returns opposite answers on consecutive nights is a test that was over-weighted, and this brief over-weighted it. Second, the destocking magnitude. SMM described the pace as 'narrowing further' and this brief carried that language into a downgrade of the inventory column. The Monday print refutes it: 917,000 mt, down 41,000 against the previous Monday's 958,000, when the prior week's Monday-to-Monday move was −21,000 and the caliber this brief quoted was −25,000. The magnitude did not narrow; it nearly doubled. The Monday series now reads 979,000, 958,000, 917,000 — the steepest single week in the sequence this brief has carried. Third, SMM's own forward band. Yesterday's brief singled out $3,100–3,250 for LME as a forecaster disagreeing with the market, and the market took eight hours to breach it: cash settled $3,327.50, up $48.00, three-month $3,320.50, a sixth consecutive higher session and a seven-week high, on a thirtieth consecutive daily draw to 254,900 mt. Fourth, and least discussed but structurally the most interesting, the cash-to-three-month spread flipped from a $0.50 contango on August 7 to a $7.00 backwardation on August 10. A backwardation is the price of immediacy, and it is the one LME datum that cannot be explained by positioning. Against four reversals stands one item that did not reverse, and it happens to be the item this brief has argued for two weeks is the real test: the physical market did not participate. A00 in Shanghai rose 10 yuan to 23,990. Central China rose 20 to 23,890. Foshan rose 60 to 24,150. Hangzhou, Chongqing and Shenyang did not move at all. Futures added 125 to 185 yuan; physical added 10 to 60. That is the definition of a move the spot market is not paying for. Two mechanical details reinforce it rather than soften it. AL2609's open interest FELL 684 on the day it cleared the range top, and its volume fell to 132,277 lots from 151,302 on August 7. A range-top breakout on lower volume and shrinking front-month open interest is short-covering in its most recognisable form; the entire net build was in AL2610, which added 11,432 in the day and 3,922 more overnight, and roll activity into October explains a large part of that. The honest reading is that the front month cleared 24,100 on fewer participants, not more. And the cost side, which this brief upgraded to bullish yesterday on a +78 weekly futures move, gave back its confirmation: alumina spot was unchanged in every single region on Monday — Shandong 2,710, Henan 2,740, Shanxi 2,730, Guangxi 2,625, Guizhou 2,765, Lianyungang 2,865 — with the SMM index at 2,696.18, down 1.34, and Australian FOB flat at $346.
