One session later the market has answered, and the answer is unambiguous: LME 3M rose 0.98% to $3,225.80 and cleared on a closing basis the $3,200 cap that had failed four times, on the same day Brent fell about 4.6% on the identical headline. Aluminum and crude decoupled outright. That is not noise, and the reason is now documented rather than inferred. Iran's foreign ministry stated on August 3 that there are no negotiations with the United States at all; what exists is an Iran–Oman discussion, in its final stages, over a temporary middle route for maritime safety, with Araghchi insisting on neither the northern nor the southern route but one respecting Iranian sovereignty. The decisive physical detail is that the middle passage has reportedly been mined and would need clearing before regular use, and the Strait remains largely closed with minimal transponder traffic. Trump's response was to call Iran's leaders unbelievably duplicitous. So the clause this brief priced on Sunday as a structural bear input turned out, within twenty-four hours, to be a claim one party denies making. The cost side explains why crude could reprice and aluminum could not: oil prices the option on flow restarting, aluminum prices the physical return of Gulf capacity, and that return is gated by war-risk insurance that has moved from about 0.25% of hull value pre-February to 3–10% by mid-July — more than $7 million for a single transit on a $100 million vessel against $250,000 before — with EGA still trucking inland from Sohar and Fujairah. A route announcement does not reset an insurance market, and it certainly does not clear mines. The correction to make is therefore specific and not cosmetic: this brief overweighted the announcement and underweighted the physical gate. But the offsetting move on the same day was equally large and points the other way, and it is domestic. SMM spot fell 130 to 140 yuan across all six quoted regions on August 3 — Shanghai to 23,500, Foshan 23,610, Wuxi 23,470, Hangzhou 23,520, Chongqing 23,490, Central China 23,330 — which is a uniform decline, not a structural one, since the Foshan-over-Shanghai spread held at 110 yuan.
