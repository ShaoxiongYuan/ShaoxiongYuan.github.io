headline: "Both of yesterday's tests were answered, and the market traded the opposite of both. The destocking question resolved emphatically bullish: SMM's Thursday print landed at 898,000 mt, DOWN 35,000 on the week against August 6's 933,000 and 19,000 below Monday's 917,000 — the steepest Thursday draw of the entire series and the first print under 900,000 mt. The basis question resolved the other way and resolved fast: SMM A00 fell 250 yuan on August 13 to 24,120 and another 250 on August 14 to 23,870, so the +115 premium recorded two days ago decayed to +15 and then to a −30 DISCOUNT against AL2608's close of 23,900 — but it did NOT snap back to the −100/−120 band this brief named as the alternative, and SMM's own South China review still places transactions at a PREMIUM of 60–100 against the 2608 contract with mainstream quotations improving from −30/−10 to −10/0. And the price fell 400 yuan anyway, because the pricing function moved to a column neither test was watching. SHFE AL2609 closed 24,090 on August 13, −255 on settlement, then 23,885 on August 14, −275 on settlement and −205 on the close, for a two-session loss of 400 from the 24,285 cycle close and a WEEK of −155 against August 7's 24,040 — an outside reversal week that printed the cycle high at 24,470 on Wednesday and the week's low at 23,810 on Friday. On this brief's own moving-average caliber, computed from ninety sessions of SHFE main-contract closes, the August 14 close sits 250 BELOW MA5 at 24,135.00 and 91 BELOW MA10 at 23,976.00 — the first close beneath MA10 since July 27 — while holding +244.25 over MA20, +537.50 over MA40 and +235.00 over MA60 at 23,650.00. LME did the same thing three sessions running: cash $3,279.00 on August 13 (−$28.50) and $3,248.00 on August 14 (−$31.00), three-month $3,275.00 then $3,246.00, a $125.00 loss from Monday's $3,373.00 peak and a weekly decline of $31.50, leaving cash below the August 7 print of $3,279.50 AND the August 6 print of $3,261.00, at its lowest since August 5. The reason is on the record and it is a supply reason. On August 12, alongside its H1 results, Emirates Global Aluminium said Al Taweelah is running at 18% of capacity and expects to reach pre-war capacity by Q1 2027, with AED 1.5 billion of restoration capex and the recycling plant at 10% ramping to full by end-2026 — against the 89 of 1,262 pots, roughly 7%, that this brief has carried since July 2, and SMM's own August 14 note calls the pace 'faster than the market had previously expected' and explicitly names it as the thing suppressing the supply-tightness premium. On August 13 the second supply item landed: Australia's largest smelter, Tomago, secured a government package of A$2.5 billion, about US$1.8 billion, over a decade from 2029, with Tomago itself investing A$1.1 billion and agreeing to curtail power at times of grid stress — Rio Tinto owns slightly more than half, Gove Aluminium Finance and Norsk Hydro hold the rest, and electricity is more than 40% of the plant's operating costs. Two smelters that the bull case had written off, one restarting faster than expected and one rescued from closure, in twenty-four hours. Against that, three things moved the bull way and are not small. LME stocks drew again on both days to 248,300 mt, a thirty-fourth consecutive draw, −8,100 on the week and the first print below 250,000. The cash-to-three-month spread flipped OUT of the $1.00 contango recorded on August 12 and back into BACKWARDATION, +$4.00 on August 13 and +$2.00 on August 14, while the flat price fell $125 — a nearby-tightness signal that directly contradicts the flat price. And macro turned decisively dovish: US July retail sales fell 0.6% against +0.1% expected, the worst month since May 2025, University of Michigan preliminary August sentiment collapsed to 51.0 from 55.2 against 54.5 expected, September hike odds fell to roughly 29% from about 34% Thursday night and about 55% a week ago, and the dollar finally broke — DXY 99.608, −0.36%. Brent closed $88.52, up about 5% on the week, with the US naval blockade of Iran's ports declared able to run 'indefinitely' and Treasury Secretary Bessent promising measures of 'economic isolation' never seen in history. The Friday night session that opened 21:00 Beijing, thirty minutes after the retail sales print, read all of that and bounced: AL2609 opened 23,960 and AL2610 23,975, ABOVE both their closes (+75, +90) and their settlements (+15, +30), highs of 24,000 and 24,005 reclaimed the handle, the low of 23,835 held above Friday's 23,810, and whole-product open interest built 12,655 to 652,352 — the largest night build of this sequence."
