Wednesday pre-market, 9:00 AM ET, thirty minutes after the April PPI print and thirty minutes before the open. The set-up overnight is the cleanest second-leg stagflation re-rate of the cycle, and the tape is showing it in size. April PPI for final demand printed plus 1.4 percent month over month, the largest single-month jump since the March 2022 supply-chain shock, against a consensus of plus 0.5 percent and our note view of plus 0.6 percent. The year-over-year line is now 6.0 percent, the highest since December 2022, with services up 1.2 percent and goods up 2.0 percent, telling the same story the April CPI told twenty-four hours ago at 3.8 percent headline and 2.8 percent core. The 10Y is at 4.45 percent in the cash session, six basis points wider on the day and twelve basis points wider on the week, with the 2Y at 3.99 percent and the curve steepening into the $42B 10Y auction at 1:00 PM. Equity futures are mixed but informative: Nasdaq 100 e-mini futures are at 29,184, up 0.30 percent, lifted by a Tencent revenue beat at plus 9.1 percent year over year, a Micron retail upgrade pre-market, and the SK Hynix and Samsung bid that resumed overnight after Monday's profit-take. S&P 500 futures are at 7,348, off 0.10 percent, with Dow futures heavy at 49,441, down 261 points or 0.50 percent on the cyclicals dragging through the PPI tape. Brent is at $107.40, off 0.35 percent and breathing a touch after Monday's plus 3.4 percent ramp, with WTI at $101.50. Senate confirmed Kevin Warsh to the Fed Board last night by 51 to 45 along largely party-line vote with only Fetterman crossing, and the chair confirmation vote lands today for the four-year concurrent term. President Trump's Air Force One departed Andrews at 4:00 AM ET for the eleven-hour leg to Beijing for tomorrow's summit opening with President Xi, with the 17 CEO delegation including Cook, Musk, and Ortberg traveling separately. Cisco prints after the close with consensus at $1.03 EPS on $15.6 billion in revenue, the first read on AI infrastructure order conversion since the Q2 print where the company raised the fiscal-year AI order book past $5 billion.

Three things to internalize before the open:

1. **The PPI shock at plus 1.4 percent month over month against the plus 0.5 percent consensus is the cleanest pipeline read on the energy passthrough and the services sticky-services dynamic, and the right structure is to hold the 2Y put spread while rolling the steepener into a 30Y short.** The PPI services line at plus 1.2 percent is the cleanest single-print read on the inflation-being-sticky thesis since the December 2022 print, with the contribution from trade services, transportation, and warehousing all elevated and the energy goods passthrough adding to the goods line. The Cleveland Nowcast for May headline now sits at 3.9 to 4.1 percent versus the consensus 3.7 percent, and the June FOMC dot plot path is the binding catalyst from here. The market priced in a 30 percent chance of a December rate hike this morning, up from 18 percent Tuesday morning and 7 percent Monday morning, and that re-pricing is exactly the right read on the Warsh hawkish anchor. We hold the 2Y June 17 4.10 / 4.30 put spread at the 17-tick mark, took the gains to 22 ticks at the 8:30 print, and we are rolling the 2s10s steepener at plus 18 basis points (up from plus 14 entry) into a 30Y June short via the TYZ pair at 4.97 percent entry. Total duration short sized at 0.30 percent DV01.

2. **The Warsh governor confirmation overnight plus the chair vote today is the structural hawkish handoff the curve has been pricing since March, and combined with the PPI shock it is the cleanest catalyst stack for a stagflation regime call in the cycle so far.** The 51-45 vote was three votes lighter on the yes side than our Monday model and four lighter than the Friday model, with Fetterman the only Democratic crossover and three Republican absences. The four-year chair vote today should clear by a similar margin around mid-afternoon, and Warsh is sworn in Friday with the Powell handoff effective on the close. The June FOMC dot plot we project, post-PPI shock, shows two fewer cuts than the March SEP, with the 2026 median moving from 75 basis points of additional easing to 25 basis points and the longer-run terminal moving from 3.00 to 3.25 percent. The Warsh installation by Friday close ends the Powell-Williams policy era cleanly and the regime read is real. We add to the SMH June 19 $310 / $330 call spread, opened Tuesday from the CEG roll at $4.20 net debit, and we open a new SPY June 30 $720 / $700 put spread at $5.10 net debit sized 0.20 percent NAV as the index-level hedge against the chair vote tape risk.

3. **The Trump-Xi summit opens tomorrow with the Iran agenda crowding tariffs and rare earths off the headline schedule, and the cleanest expression of the deliverable risk is long BA into the call vertical we have held since Wednesday plus a tactical China consumer pair via JD long against BABA short.** Air Force One departed at 4:00 AM ET, with the 17 CEO delegation including Musk and Cook traveling on a separate Boeing, and the CSIS preview pegs the aircraft purchase deliverable at 200 to 500 units. The Iran agenda is the binding catalyst: Trump is expected to renew the call for China to join the international Hormuz operation, and Beijing has so far resisted, which sets up the cleanest signaling read on whether China will tolerate the Brent passthrough into Q3 or whether Beijing will lean on Tehran in exchange for tariff relief.
