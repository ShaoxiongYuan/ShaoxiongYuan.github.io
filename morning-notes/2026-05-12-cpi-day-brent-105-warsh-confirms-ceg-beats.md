We hold the 2Y June 17 4.10 / 4.30 put spread at 12 ticks entry, mark 17 ticks Friday close, and we hold size at 0.25 percent NAV into the print rather than pressing.

We are running the 2s10s steepener via the OFR pair at plus 8 basis points entry from May 7, mark plus 14 basis points.
