## What Changed Overnight

- **Diplomacy gap widened:** public messaging between Washington, Tehran, and Jerusalem diverged again.
- **Energy risk re-priced:** Kuwait and Iraq flow uncertainty kept a firm floor under crude.
- **Rates remained hostile:** ECB commentary reinforced global inflation stickiness and delayed easing expectations.
- **Quant de-risking accelerated:** high-beta growth sleeves saw mechanical reduction, amplifying intraday downside.

## Trade Focus

1. **Keep defense and energy as macro hedges** while ceasefire probability remains unstable.
2. **Prefer AI infrastructure over long-duration software** in a high-rate tape.
3. **Use rallies to trim weak consumer beta** until confidence and fuel trends stabilize.

## Bottom Line

The market is no longer willing to pay for optimistic ceasefire headlines without verifiable progress. With oil back above $106, volatility firm, and policy tone still hawkish, risk assets remain headline-fragile. Positioning should stay barbelled: structural AI winners on one side, geopolitical/inflation hedges on the other.
