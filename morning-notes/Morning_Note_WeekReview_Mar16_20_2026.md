OIL & ENERGY CRISIS, THE SUPPLY SHOCK IS NOW STRUCTURAL
Brent crude rose 7.6% this week to $112.10, its fifth consecutive weekly gain and highest level since 2022. The supply picture deteriorated dramatically:
Kuwait Mina al-Ahmadi: 730K bpd offline after IRGC-linked attack set refineries ablaze. No timeline for restart.
Iraq Basra: Force majeure declared on crude exports. 1M+ bpd at risk.
UAE Fujairah: Terminal fire from Wednesday retaliation still not fully contained. Storage capacity compromised.
South Pars: Israel struck Iran’s largest gas field Thursday. Iran exports 5% of global LNG. Gas prices spiked.
Hormuz: Partially disrupted. Iran targeting Gulf infrastructure directly rather than closing the strait outright.
Sanctions relief: Treasury Sec. Bessent announced lifting sanctions on 140M barrels of stranded Iranian oil. Short-term supply boost, but drops in the bucket vs. 2M+ bpd disrupted.

Goldman Sachs says oil may stay in triple digits for years if the war continues. The energy crisis is no longer a tail risk,it is the base case.
