## Key Drivers

- **Stagflation fears intensified:** inflation prints stayed hot while growth expectations softened.
- **China trade retaliation:** tariff/counter-tariff headlines worsened global demand visibility.
- **Oil re-spike:** crude re-approached crisis highs, pressuring margins and consumer spending.
- **Cross-asset stress:** equity weakness paired with higher yields and firmer safe-haven demand.

## Positioning Notes

1. **Maintain high-quality AI/infra exposure, but hedge beta** with index puts or defensive longs.
2. **Keep commodity and defense allocations as convex hedges** to geopolitical and inflation risk.
3. **Avoid chasing oversold bounces** until core inflation and trade headlines stabilize.

## Bottom Line

The market is now pricing a stagflation regime rather than a temporary geopolitical dislocation. With all major indices under correction pressure, crude near $111, and volatility above 30, capital preservation and selective risk-taking should dominate portfolio construction into month-end.
